Our Programs

Three programs, one through-line.

Financial institutions carry risk through dependencies they cannot see and cannot directly govern. Provenance Risk Research is organizing three programs to change that.

Research

Peer-reviewable working papers and journal submissions on dependency-level risk, software supply chain exposure, open source maintainer concentration, and operational resilience in regulated financial services. One paper published; Paper 2 in drafting.

Case Studies

Planned program of structured analyses applying our published frameworks to third-party risk and software supply chain incidents in regulated industries. First case studies forthcoming.

Education

Planned program of diagnostic frameworks, instructional materials, and case-based curricula for third-party risk teams, regulators, and the academic community studying these systems.

Forthcoming
Working Paper No. 2
The Unmanaged Attack Surface: Why Vendor-Level Attestation Misses Software Supply Chain Risk

Targets the Journal of Operational Risk, 2026. Uses a diagnostic-question methodology to reveal gaps in SOC 2 and similar attestation frameworks for the software supply chain conditions that cause correlated failures. Includes a ten-jurisdiction regulatory survey and introduces the Concentration Risk Index.
