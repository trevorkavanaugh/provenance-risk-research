Open Datasets

Research conclusions should be auditable.

Provenance Risk Research releases the data and methodology underlying our work as open datasets, under permissive licenses, with reproducible documentation.

Our datasets serve practitioners building diagnostic tools, researchers extending our findings, and regulators assessing the evidentiary base of policy recommendations. Each dataset is versioned, methodology-documented, and hosted on GitHub for version control and collaborative improvement.

Maintainer Burnout Open Dataset

Software supply chain risk begins upstream of the software supply chain - in the sustainability of the open source projects that modern systems depend on. A single burned-out maintainer of a widely-adopted package is a dependency-level risk that no entity-based third-party risk framework will surface.

This dataset catalogs signals of maintainer sustainability across a corpus of open source projects, supporting research, tooling, and policy work on the resilience of the upstream open source ecosystem.
